Frame 08 / Field Notes

Asked &
answered.

Q.01What's actually included for $100?

A 15-minute studio session in Orlando, one professionally-retouched final headshot delivered as a high-res JPEG, and a 5–7 day turnaround. One look, one background. That's the SKU. no hidden per-image upsells.

Q.02How does the spot cap actually work?

100 spots total. Sessions are scheduled across multiple shoot days through the booking calendar. When the 100th spot is claimed, the page closes and the next promotion is announced separately. The counter at the top of this page is updated by hand as bookings come in. no fake countdown timers.

Q.03Where exactly is the studio?

Sessions are held at an Orlando studio at 5644 Commerce Dr, Unit A (SoDo neighborhood, just south of Downtown Orlando, ~10 minutes from Lake Eola, Thornton Park, and Baldwin Park). Free on-site parking. Exact arrival instructions are sent in the booking confirmation.

Q.04What should I wear?

One outfit you'd wear to your most important meeting. Solid colors photograph cleanest. A two-page wardrobe guide PDF is sent the same day you book. bring one option you love. We're shooting one look, so don't overthink it.

Q.05Can my team book together?

Yes. Teams of 3 or more can request adjacent slots on the same day. mention it in the booking notes and we'll line them up. Each person still counts as one of the 100 spots, so move quickly if your team is bigger than five.

Q.06What if I want more than one final image?

The $100 release is one final, period. that's the whole point of the SKU. If you want more, you have two paths: (a) add additional retouched images at $45 each after your session, or (b) book the full Starter Branding package. $649 for a 2-hour shoot, 10 fully edited images, 2 outfits, studio + editing all bundled. Full pricing on glpxstudio.com.

Q.07Is this a discounted version of your branding packages?

No. and that distinction matters. The Starter Branding package is $649 for a 2-hour shoot with 10 fully edited images and 2 outfits. The Signature Branding package is $1,100 for 4 hours, 15 edited images, 4 outfits. This release is 15 minutes, 1 final, capped at 100. Same studio, same lighting, same retouching standard. different deliverable. The $100 release is a one-time, capped event.

Q.08Can I reschedule?

Yes. one free reschedule with at least 48 hours notice. Inside 48 hours, the deposit is non-refundable but can be transferred to another open spot if availability permits. If you're a no-show, the spot is forfeited.

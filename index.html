FAQ.

Q · 01 What if I'm not photogenic?

Almost no one thinks they are. The job of a portrait photographer is to know which side of your face you like, where the light should be, and what to say between frames so you stop holding your breath. That's what the 15 minutes are for. You don't need to be photogenic. You just need to show up.

Q · 02 What should I wear?

Solid colors over patterns. Something that fits well at the shoulders. A jacket or layer adds structure for business-style headshots. Once you book, you'll get a short style guide with examples, plus a "what not to wear" note.

Q · 03 How long until I get the photos?

3 to 5 business days. You'll receive your one fully edited, print-ready portrait as a high-resolution download, ready for LinkedIn, your website, your bio page, and any print collateral.

Q · 04 Where is the studio?

Sessions are at Santoku Studios in Orlando, FL. You'll get the full address, parking notes, and a map link in your booking confirmation.

Q · 05 Can I add extra edited images?

Yes. Additional retouched, print-ready images are $45 each. You can pick favorites from your session after the shoot. If you want a broader branding setup (multiple outfits, more frames, more time), see the full price guide at glpxstudio.com.
